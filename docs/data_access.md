# Data access

The clinical records are held by VetCompass Australia (University of Sydney) and were collected under The University of Sydney Human Research Ethics Committee approval (project number 2013/919). They cannot be shared publicly because free-text notes may be re-identifiable. Access is subject to VetCompass Australia's governance arrangements (enquiries: info@vetcompass.com.au).

This repository contains no clinical notes, labels, predictions or adapter weights. The reported-figure check (`vetnutrilm reproduce`) requires authorised access to the saved run outputs.
